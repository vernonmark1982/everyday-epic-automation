## The real driver

The thumbnail is doing almost nothing for this video — a 29/100 score on a
video that still did 1.26M views and 4,446 VPH proves the click isn't
coming from the static thumbnail at all. For 6-second Shorts, the feed
autoplays before a viewer consciously evaluates the thumbnail; the title
text + the "#blowup" tag riding existing hashtag momentum is carrying
almost all of the CTR (91/100), and the *format itself* (instant payoff,
no scroll-away window) is carrying the rest via algorithmic push once
watch-through rate is high.

## Takeaway for our own content

For ultra-short (<10s) Shorts in this format, stop over-investing in
thumbnail design — a generic, sharp, in-context frame is enough. Put the
effort budget into the title's curiosity gap and the first-second hook
instead. This is the opposite of the standard advice for long-form YouTube,
where thumbnail quality is usually the single biggest CTR lever — the
autopsy shows the lever moves with format length, not just niche.
