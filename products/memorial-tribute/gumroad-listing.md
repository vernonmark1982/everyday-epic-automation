**You just got the call. The service is in days. And everyone is looking at you.**

Someone has to write the obituary. Someone has to stand up and speak. Someone has to lay out the program. In the worst week of your year, those tasks land on the people carrying the most.

Search for help and you'll find generic templates that sound like they were written by a form, and advice articles telling you to "speak from the heart" — as if the problem were sincerity and not the blank page.

The Memorial Tribute Kit is the third thing: **complete, warm, human writing — organized by your relationship to the person** — that works as-is at midnight, or shapes into something more personal if you have the time and strength.

**20 eulogy scripts. 10 obituary templates. 5 program layouts.** Instant download. Available tonight.

### What's Inside

**20 Eulogy Scripts — By Relationship**
For a parent, spouse, sibling, child, grandparent, close friend, mentor, colleague, and more — including dedicated sudden-loss versions. Each script is complete: opening, remembrance structure, the turn toward gratitude, and closing. Read it as-is with your details, or use it as scaffolding for your own memories.

**10 Obituary Templates**
- Short: 100–150 words for a program insert or announcement
- Standard: 250–300 words for the funeral home bulletin
- Extended: 400–600 words for a celebration of life program

Each opens with who this person was — never with how they died. Warm and human, never form-letter.

**5 Memorial Program Layouts**
Complete order-of-service templates in both religious and secular versions: processional, readings, music placement, tribute and eulogy slots, committal, and reception details. Print-ready PDFs a funeral home or print shop can use directly.

**"What to Say When You Have No Words" — Emergency Guide**
For the person handed the microphone who never expected to hold it. Opening remarks when you're not a speaker. Closing words when no officiant is present. In-the-moment prompts for the hardest five minutes of a hard day.

**Sudden Loss Supplement**
Dedicated scripts and obituary language for unexpected deaths — accidents, sudden illness, and loss without warning. Different emotional register. Different framing. The standard scripts don't carry this weight. These do.

**Complicated Relationship Guide**
How to adapt any script when the relationship was complicated — estranged family, blended families, chosen family, or a relationship that was loving and difficult at once. A tribute can be honest and kind at the same time. This guide shows where that line is.
