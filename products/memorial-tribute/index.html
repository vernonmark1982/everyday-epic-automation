Why This Exists

Grief and a deadline
should never share the same week.

But they always do. A service comes together in 48 to 72 hours. In that window, someone has to write an obituary for the paper and the program. Someone has to stand up and speak. Someone has to lay out the order of service. And the people carrying those tasks are the same people carrying the loss.

Search for help and you'll find two things: generic templates that sound like they were written by a form, and advice articles telling you to "speak from the heart" — as if the problem were sincerity and not the blank page.

The Memorial Tribute Kit is the third thing: complete, warm, human writing — organized by your relationship to the person — that you can use as-is at midnight, or shape into something more personal if you have the time and strength. Either way, the page is never blank again.
