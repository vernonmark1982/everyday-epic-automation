## TRACK 01 — "Blackout" (the wind-down opener)

**SONG TITLE:**
Blackout

**STYLE:**
Cinematic jazz-ambient at 66 BPM, engineered as a wind-down descent. Warm upright bass sustained on root and fifth with a slow, breath-paced attack; brushed-drum texture at the very edge of audibility, more felt than counted, no defined backbeat. Rhodes electric piano voiced in wide, low maj7 and min9 chords with the high tines rolled off so no partial rings above 4kHz. A single muted-trumpet motif enters far back in the mix, reverb-drenched, descending through a perfect-fifth fall then resolving down by whole step. Harmonic motion is a slow ii–V–I in a low register that never fully brightens — each resolution lands softer than the last. Vinyl-warmth room texture around -30dB as a familiar acoustic cocoon. Everything descends: tempo feel, melodic contour, and brightness all trend downward across the track toward stillness. No percussion transients sharp enough to startle. Design target: parasympathetic settling — invite the resting pulse to follow the tempo down.

## TRACK 03 — "Seventh Floor Quiet" (the deep-sleep centerpiece)

**SONG TITLE:**
Seventh Floor Quiet

**STYLE:**
Cinematic jazz-ambient at 62 BPM, the deep-hold centerpiece of the collection. Grand piano this time instead of Rhodes, played rubato with wide-spaced low min9 and 11th voicings, sustain pedal held so the harmonics blur into a warm cloud, high frequencies rolled off below 4kHz. Upright bass walks almost imperceptibly — one note every two bars, deep and round, 40–60Hz felt in the chest not heard. A single sustained string-pad undertone, ribbon-mic intimacy, no vibrato. The harmony sits in a low modal space — Dorian color, no strong leading tone, no resolution that would create a "wake" pull; it floats and holds rather than moving forward. One muted-trumpet phrase appears once, at the two-thirds point, descends, and does not return. The track's job is to sustain the deepest part of the sleep descent — minimal event density, nothing that segments the attention, a continuous slow-evolving field. Design target: sustained parasympathetic hold, no arousal spikes.

## TRACK 10 — "First Light Before the Shift" (the pre-shift reset / re-ascent)

**SONG TITLE:**
First Light Before the Shift

**STYLE:**
Cinematic jazz-ambient that reverses the descent — a gentle re-ascent from 60 BPM rising to 72 BPM across its length, engineered to wake the body without a jolt. Opens where the deep-sleep tracks end: low Rhodes maj7, sustained bass, near-stillness. Across the track, elements are added one at a time — a soft brushed pulse finds a gentle backbeat, the Rhodes voicings climb from low to mid register, a muted trumpet motif ascends by step (the only ascending contour in the entire collection, and deliberate). Brightness is allowed to increase gradually but still ceilings below the harsh alerting band — this is a sunrise, not an alarm. Harmony moves from the low modal float of the sleep tracks toward a warm, resolved maj7 that finally, for the first time on the album, brightens. The arrangement blooms slowly and never spikes. Design target: gentle cortisol-friendly awakening — invite the system up rather than forcing it.

No alarm-like transients, no sudden dynamic jumps, no bright supersaw, no EDM build/drop, no trap hats, no aggressive kick, no white noise, no vocal chops, no new-age flutes, no cliché sunrise-pad swell, no key-change gimmick, no crescendo that startles, no percussion sharp enough to jolt, no rising synth riser/whoosh FX.
