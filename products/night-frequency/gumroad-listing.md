---

### How It's Engineered

These are the design targets every track was built to — the engineering intent, not a medical promise:

- **60–72 BPM** — slow enough to invite the resting pulse to follow it down. Every track descends rather than holds.
- **Descending harmonic contours** — melodies fall rather than climb. No rising phrases that pull attention back up.
- **No frequency peaks above 4kHz** — the bright, alerting frequencies that snap you awake are rolled off. Nothing in the mix says "pay attention."
- **Jazz and soul DNA** — warm Rhodes, upright bass, brushed textures. The OBSIDIAN HOUR sound. Not a hum. Music.

---
