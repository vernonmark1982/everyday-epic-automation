### What's Inside

**20 Complete Speech Scripts — By Role and Tone**
- Best Man ×4: funny, heartfelt, the brother, the oldest friend
- Maid of Honor ×4: funny, heartfelt, the sister, the oldest friend
- Father of the Bride, Mother of the Bride
- Father of the Groom, Mother of the Groom
- Sibling, grandparent, oldest friend, co-worker, and more

Each script is complete and timed to a 3–4 minute delivery, with cut points marked if you need it shorter. Use it as-is with your details, or treat it as scaffolding for your own stories.

**50 Opening Lines**
The first 15 seconds decide whether the room is with you. 50 tested openers organized by style — funny, warm, self-deprecating, formal — each one swappable into any script.

**30 Toasts & Closing Lines**
The landing matters as much as the takeoff. Classic, modern, funny, and tear-jerker closers, so the glass raise gets the moment it deserves.

**The "Funny Without Bombing" Humor Guide**
The rules of wedding humor: which stories work in a room of 150 people who don't know you, which ones die, and the topics that are always off the table (exes, money, anything the couple hasn't told both families). Includes the roast-to-toast pivot — the one structural move every great best man speech shares.

**The Delivery Guide**
The 24-hour countdown for people who don't speak in public and would rather not start now: how to practice, how to manage nerves, pacing marks, where to look, what to do with the drink in your hand, and how to handle the mic.

**The "Asked to Speak Tonight" Emergency Section**
Surprise toasts happen — at the rehearsal dinner, at the reception, when the DJ hands you the mic. Three 60-second speeches you can personalize on your phone in the hallway.
