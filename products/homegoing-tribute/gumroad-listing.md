When someone in your family is called home, you have a narrow window to put together something worthy of who they were. You search for templates. ---

### What Makes This Different

A homegoing is not a funeral.

A homegoing opens with celebration: *"We come today not in sorrow alone, but in the knowledge that our loved one has gone home."* A homegoing carries gospel, praise, and the certainty of faith. A homegoing is structured around the community that loved this person — not a standard order of service.

Every script, template, and layout in this kit was written in that register. With that understanding. For the families, church secretaries, pastors, and community members who carry these moments and deserve tools worthy of them.

No existing template product gets this right. We know — we checked every one.

### What's Inside

**20 Tribute Scripts — By Relationship**
Organized by who you're honoring: parent, sibling, child, spouse, close friend, mentor, elder, sudden loss, grandmother, grandfather, godparent, and more. Each script is complete and ready to read aloud as-is, or personalize with the specific details of their life. Includes opening remarks, celebration of life framing, and closing.

**10 Obituary Templates**
Short (program insert, 100–150 words), standard (funeral home bulletin, 250–300 words), and extended (celebration of life program, 400–600 words). Different tones for different contexts. All written in homegoing register — opening with who this person was, not how they died.

**5 Homegoing Service Program Layouts**
Complete order of service templates for Black church homegoing services. Includes: processional, scripture reading, musical selection placeholders, tribute placement, responsive reading, obituary placement, remarks from clergy, recessional, and repast details. Print-ready PDF format.

**"What to Say When You Have No Words" — Emergency Guide**
For the person who has to speak and cannot find the language. Opening remarks when you're not a speaker. Closing benedictions when no clergy is present. In-the-moment prompts for the hardest five minutes of a hard day.

**Sudden Loss Supplement**
Scripts and obituary language specifically for unexpected deaths — accidents, sudden illness, violence, suicide. Different emotional weight. Different level of grief. Different framing. The standard scripts don't carry this. These do.

**Relationship Modifier Guide**
Instructions for adapting any script to complex family structures: estranged relationships, blended families, chosen family, multi-generational loss, deaths where the relationship was complicated. Because real families don't fit a standard template, and the tribute still needs to honor what was real.
