The Difference That Matters

A Homegoing Is Not
a Funeral.

The memorial industry has one template. It was written by and for a specific cultural tradition. When a Black family reaches for it at the worst moment of their year, what they get back is language that doesn't fit — formal where it should be warm, mournful where it should be triumphant, generic where it should carry the weight of a life that meant something specific.

Generic Memorial Template
  • Opens with loss and condolences
  • Formal, distant register
  • No celebration of life framing
  • Ignores church and faith language
  • No "going home" or homegoing framing
  • Writes for the grieving, not the celebrant
Homegoing Tribute Kit
  • Opens with celebration: "We come today not in sorrow alone..."
  • Warm, full register — the voice of the community
  • Homegoing as transition, not ending
  • Praise, gospel, and faith woven throughout
  • 20 relationship-specific scripts (parent, sibling, sudden loss)
  • Writes for the dignity the person deserved
