### What's Inside

**The 60-Page Nightshift Wealth System PDF**
The complete budgeting framework rebuilt for rotation schedules. Covers rotation-aware expense tracking, biweekly irregular pay alignment, overtime income management, shift differential accounting, and the off-hours spending patterns that quietly drain shift worker accounts. Not a generic guide renamed for night shift workers — built from the ground up for how you actually live, work, and get paid.

**Rotation Budget Tracker (Google Sheets)**
Templates for 3×12, 4×10, and 2-2-3 rotation schedules. Enter your pay cycle dates and the tracker maps your bills, savings targets, and spending categories to your actual rotation — not a Monday calendar. Includes separate tabs for regular pay, overtime tracking, and shift differential calculation.

**12-Week Shift Worker Habit Tracker**
One financial habit per two-week rotation. Designed around how shift workers actually have energy — not daily streaks that collapse the moment you work three nights in a row. 12 weeks of small, consistent moves builds more than any daily tracking app.

**Overtime & Shift Differential Calculator**
The tool that tells you exactly what your "good month" paycheck means for your baseline savings goal. So when overtime disappears, you don't disappear financially with it.

**Off-Hours Spending Audit Template**
The 20-minute worksheet that maps exactly where money leaves your account after night shifts and during the dead hours when your impulse spending is highest — 7 AM drive-throughs, 3 AM online orders, off-rotation convenience spending. Most shift workers find $200–400/month they can't account for.

**Bill Renegotiation Scripts**
The exact words to use when calling each biller to move your due dates to align with your rotation pay cycle. Most billers say yes. Most shift workers never know to ask. These scripts do the work for you.
